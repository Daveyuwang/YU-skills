# Research Figure: Source Notes

Source: YU's research-figure preferences and Figure 1 revision history supplied
in the project conversation. The [research-figures skill](../skills/research-figures/SKILL.md)
distills reusable preferences for visual hierarchy, typography, color, truthful
data and flow, editable output, and iterative review. The
[pipeline reference](../skills/research-figures/references/pipeline-diagrams.md)
contains the conditional guidance for method and interaction diagrams.

The defaults retain YU's preference for no internal title, no panel letters,
no commas in figure text, and original Apple emoji when emoji are appropriate.
They remain subject to the current brief and venue; exact quotations and data
must stay faithful to their sources.

The Figure 1 history contains project-specific model names, actors, examples,
connector routes, font choices, sizes, and version-specific overrides. These
were not made universal rules. The reusable lessons are to follow the actual
method and evidence, prioritize the core module, place feedback locally, keep
examples subordinate, and inspect the rendered result at its final size.
