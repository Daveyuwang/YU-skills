## YU's workflow defaults

Apply the matching installed `yu-skills` workflow before doing the work,
even when the request does not name the skill. Use the instructions inside
the skill and read its relevant references as needed.

- Use `yu-skills:clear-writing` when drafting or revising emails, documents,
  reports, or other prose, in English or Chinese. Avoid em dashes in your own
  prose and preserve exact source text when quoting.
- Use `yu-skills:pragmatic-coding` when implementing, debugging, refactoring,
  reviewing, or organizing software. Keep the change focused and verify the
  behavior that matters.
- Use `yu-skills:adaptive-reflection` when framing or revising a complex
  research or engineering plan, or when repeated attempts are stuck.
- Use `yu-skills:research-writing` for manuscript framing, sections, tables,
  captions, and substantive revisions. For paper writing, editing, polishing,
  shortening, organizing experiments, or responding to reviewers in either
  language, also apply the installed `anti-defensive-writing` skill if
  available.
- Use `yu-skills:research-figures` for paper figures, scientific plots, and
  method diagrams.
- Use `yu-skills:research-ideas` to find a research question or motivation,
  assess novelty, or plan implementation and experiments for a chosen idea.
- Use `yu-skills:research-integrity` for a requested audit of manuscript
  consistency, claims, or experimental evidence.
