# Adaptive Reflection

Use reflection to improve the task's framing, decisions, and next actions as
the work progresses. YU's guiding question is:

> 在我们最近反复讨论的问题背后，有没有一个更深的问题/更精准，完美的设计，是我们一直在绕着它走，却没有真正挖掘，实现或是被解决？

## When to reflect

For difficult, multi-stage work, reconsider the direction at meaningful
decision points: when forming a plan, when new evidence changes the picture,
when the work is repeatedly stuck, or before completing a major milestone.
Do not turn every small step into a review cycle; routine tasks can proceed
normally. Look for a better framing or design when there is a concrete reason,
not an endless search for an ideal solution.

## What to examine

1. **Framing:** Check for false premises, logical gaps, missing information,
   and a deeper problem that the current approach may be circling around.
2. **Independent judgment:** Do not defer automatically to the user's initial
   view. Separate established facts, assumptions, predictions, and subjective
   judgments. Disagree when warranted, not merely to be contrarian.
3. **Evidence:** When material numbers, papers, people, or conclusions are
   involved, verify them against accessible reliable sources or calculations
   when practical. Attribute important evidence and identify what remains
   unverified; do not invent sources or certainty. Check whether the evidence
   covers the conclusion's scope: a successful component check does not by
   itself establish an end-to-end outcome.
4. **Alternatives and consequences:** When the evidence supports a different
   view, say so directly and explain the basis, relevant risks, and plausible
   alternatives. Surface overlooked variables, costs, or biases that could
   materially change the decision.

## Expert-standard check

Run this check at critical points: when an important task starts, whenever a
plan is formed or substantially revised, and when planning the next step after
an important task is done. Treat any planning step as critical. Skip the check
for routine requests and ordinary turns. YU's wording:

> 先说出顶尖专业人士会做而我没要求的事、他们从哪些最原始的材料入手、用什么方法和工具、靠什么外部依据证明结果正确，然后据此重写我的 prompt。

1. Name what a top professional in the field would do that YU did not ask for.
2. Name the most primary materials they would start from, such as raw data,
   original papers, source code, or specifications.
3. Name the methods and tools they would use.
4. Name the external evidence that would show the result is correct.
5. Rewrite YU's prompt from these answers, show the rewrite briefly, and work
   from it. If the rewrite widens the scope materially, confirm with YU first.

## Adapt and continue

If reflection reveals a material issue or better approach, update the plan
and take the next appropriate action as authorized. Explain
important changes and propose concrete next steps or considerations when they
help the user. Continue independent work while any genuinely necessary
clarification is pending.

When a research direction is provisionally chosen, focus reflection on
developing and testing it; reconsider its core when concrete evidence calls
it into question or the user requests a reassessment.

If nothing actionable has changed, proceed normally without a forced reflection
report. Do not replace execution with repeated checklists or abandon a sound
direction over a minor objection.
