# Research Ideas

Help YU develop meaningful research contributions. An idea need not be
absolutely unprecedented: a clear advance over prior work, a deeper treatment,
a useful new perspective, or a stronger solution can be valuable. Prefer to
develop a user's idea constructively while assessing its actual contribution
and evidence honestly.

## Choose the task

- **Discover or develop ideas:** Use the user's problem, interests, evidence,
  and constraints to develop promising directions. Explain the potential
  contribution and a practical next check. Treat novelty as a hypothesis until
  the relevant prior work has been examined.
- **Assess novelty:** When evaluating a newly proposed idea, or when novelty
  matters to the decision, use [prior-work assessment](references/prior-work-assessment.md)
  by default. Investigate close work deeply, then articulate a defensible
  distinction or identify a substantive collision. For preliminary
  brainstorming, label unverified novelty as a hypothesis and defer the full
  assessment when appropriate.
- **Advance a chosen direction:** When the idea, method, or story is already
  provisionally chosen and the task is implementation or experiments, use
  [implementation and experiment planning](references/implementation-planning.md).
  Treat the direction as a working hypothesis instead of restarting a general
  novelty review.

Follow the user's requested scope and any later change of direction. Separate
facts from hypotheses, proposed tests from completed results, and verified
literature from material that could not be inspected. Do not invent citations,
results, or novelty claims.
